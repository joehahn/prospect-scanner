# Notes the operator sent to people like this one

**Version 1, 2026-10-05.**

*v1: first version. The fixed example notes above are cut to a few anchors, and
the rest of the examples are chosen for each draft from the notes the operator
actually sent, most similar recipient first.*

Below are notes the operator sent to people most like the one you are writing to:
a similar seat, a similar kind of firm, the same channel or offer where possible.
Each shows what the drafting step wrote, what he asked to have changed (if
anything), and what he actually sent.

**What he sent is the example.** The draft is there only so you can see what he
removed, added or reworded. Where the two differ, his version is the voice; the
draft is what the voice is not. Where he sent the draft unchanged, it was right
as written.

**Take the voice, never the facts.** These went to other people, at other firms,
about their circumstances. Nothing in them is evidence about this recipient. A
fact, a name, an event or a number from an example must never appear in this
note. The evidence for this note is in the dossier, and only there.

**Do not copy sentences.** These recipients share sectors and conferences, and a
sentence that recurs across notes reads as a mail merge to anyone who sees two.
Match the shape, register and length; write this note's sentences fresh.

Where an example's channel or offer differs from this note's, follow this note's
channel rules and offer, and take only the register from the example.
