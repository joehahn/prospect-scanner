# Follow-up test arm: one proof, one easy next step

**Version 1, 2026-11-12.**

*v1: the operator approved an alternating test of the message after a silent
accept. Half of those messages are drafted as before (draft-cold-note, "The
message after an accepted connection": an open door, no tactic). This half
replaces the open door with one proof and one easy next step. Both outside
reviewers, from two different makers, found that every opportunity on record
came from a note that stated an offer, and that the people who did reply were
sent proof (case-study links) only after asking for it. About 0 of the
open-door follow-ups had drawn a reply. The test decides; neither arm is
assumed better. The drafter's own instructions are frozen and are not edited
for this.*

---

THIS MESSAGE IS IN A TEST ARM. For this message only, the following replaces
two rules in "The message after an accepted connection": "An open door, and
nothing to do" and "No tactic". Everything else in that section still holds:
why he asked to connect, the offer for the first time, sized to their seat,
and one plain note in his voice.

- **One proof, with its link.** Choose the one item from CITABLE PROOF POINTS
  closest to this person's work, and say in a few plain words what it shows
  that they would care about. Give its URL. Only one; never a list. If none is
  close to their work, use none, and say so in the NOTES.
- **One easy next step, as a question.** One short question they can answer
  in a few words without a meeting: whether a short write-up or a closer look
  at that kind of work would be useful to them, or whether something else is
  higher on their list right now. Never a call, a meeting, a demo or a referral;
  those wait until they have replied.
- Keep the whole note short: four to six sentences.

In the NOTES, say "TEST ARM: proof_step", which proof you chose and why.
