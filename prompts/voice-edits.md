# What the operator changed in notes to people like this one

**Version 1, 2026-10-05.**

*v1: first version. In place of whole example notes, the sentences the operator
rewrote, cut or added in notes to the most similar recipients on this channel,
and what his plain-word instructions changed.*

Most drafts go out close to how they were written. What he changes is the
lesson. Below, for notes to people most like this recipient, are:

- **REWROTE**: a sentence as drafted, and as he sent it. His version is the
  voice; the drafted one is what the voice is not.
- **CUT**: a sentence he removed. Notes like this one do not need it.
- **ADDED**: a sentence he wrote that no draft had.
- **HE ASKED**: an instruction he gave in plain words, and the sentences it
  changed.

"(the opening)" marks the sentence after the greeting, the one he changes most.

**Learn the direction of the edits, then write this note so it would not need
them.** If he keeps shortening openings, write a short one. If he keeps cutting a
kind of sentence, do not write it.

**Take the voice, never the facts.** These sentences went to other people about
their circumstances. No fact, name, event or number from them may appear in this
note; the evidence for this note is in the dossier, and only there. Do not copy
sentences either: match the direction of the change, and write this note's
sentences fresh.
